The Process

A simple system.
A repeatable result.

Four steps. One outcome: a monetization engine that runs while you create.

01

Audience Audit

We go deep on your audience — their pain points, purchase behaviour, what they're already asking you for. We map every angle of untapped revenue hiding in your existing content and community.

Deliverable: Full audit report + monetization roadmap.

02

Product Build

We design and build the offer from the ground up. Course architecture, community structure, coaching program, or digital download — positioned and packaged to match exactly what your audience wants to buy.

Deliverable: Complete offer + sales assets.

03

Launch System

A full pre-launch and launch execution plan. Warm-up content strategy, email sequences, landing page copy, checkout flow, and a day-by-day launch calendar — all built and ready to deploy.

Deliverable: Turnkey launch system, ready to activate.

04

Launch & Optimize

We go live, monitor every metric, and optimize in real time. Post-launch, we analyze the data, run split tests, and build the repeat-launch playbook so the next one performs even better.

Deliverable: Post-launch report + evergreen optimization plan.

Revenue Model

We share in the win.
Nothing more.

Our revenue share structure is based on launch size. Larger launches mean more of our time and capital — but you always keep the majority.

90/10

Starter Tier

For first-time launches or smaller audiences. You keep 90% of every dollar generated. Perfect for testing and validating your first offer.

80/20

Standard Tier

Our most common engagement. Full offer build, launch system, and optimization cycle. For creators with an established audience ready to scale.

70/30

Growth Tier

For high-volume partnerships with deep ongoing optimization, multi-offer ecosystems, and full funnel ownership. Built for serious scale.
