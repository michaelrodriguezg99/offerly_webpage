Fitness & Wellness · Instagram · 120K Followers

$47K launch in 14 days.

Sofia had the following, the credibility, and the DMs — but no system to turn it into revenue. We built her first offer in six weeks and launched to her existing audience.

The Situation

Sofia was generating around $1,200/month from brand deals and affiliate links on 120K Instagram followers. She'd been approached by coaches but couldn't afford a four-figure monthly retainer. Her audience was engaged, loyal, and constantly asking for more structured guidance.


What We Built

An 8-week transformation program delivered via a private community platform. We wrote every email in the launch sequence (21 emails over 14 days), built a custom landing page, and created a scarcity-driven cart-close strategy that generated a massive spike on day 12.

Personal Finance · YouTube · 83K Subscribers

$0 → $30K MRR in 90 days.

Marcus was sitting on an engaged YouTube audience with no monetization beyond ads. We audited, built, and launched a subscription community that now generates predictable monthly revenue.

The Situation

Marcus was earning $2,100/month from YouTube ad revenue on 83K subscribers. His comment sections were full of detailed financial questions — a clear signal that his audience wanted deeper guidance than free YouTube content could provide.


What We Built

A monthly membership community ($49/month) with a live Q&A component, investment templates, and an archive of decision frameworks. We built a 3-tier pricing structure (community, coaching calls, done-for-you analysis) and launched with a founding member window at a 30% discount.

The Results

Month 1 MRR $14,700
Month 3 MRR $30,400
Founding Members 302
Churn Rate (M1) 3.2%
Audience Growth +18%
